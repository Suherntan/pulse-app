# Speech – When to invest in Unit Trust, when to invest in PRS

- **Created:** 27 Oct 2026 · about 3 minutes · for a client talk or a short video
- **Reminder:** selling unit trusts needs a **UTC licence**, and selling PRS needs a **PRS licence** (both from FIMM). Without one, keep it as general education.

---

## The speech

**Opening (the question)**
"Many people ask me: should I put my money in a unit trust or in PRS? And *when* is the right time?
My honest answer: it's not about guessing the market. It's about **your goal, when you need the money, and your tax**."

**Step 1 – Before both: your safety net**
"Before any investment, keep **6 months of expenses** in cash. If something happens, you won't be forced to sell your investment at a bad time."

**Step 2 – When PRS is the right tool**
"PRS is a **retirement-only** account. It's the right time for PRS when:
1. **You pay income tax.** You can claim up to **RM3,000 a year** in tax relief. If your tax rate is 11%, that's about **RM330 back** in tax. At 25%, it's **RM750**.
2. **The money is truly for retirement.** 70% is locked until age 55, so it's good for people who find it hard *not* to spend.
3. **It's near the end of the year.** Put it in **before 31 December** so it counts for this year's tax.
In short: **PRS = retirement money + tax saving.**"

**Step 3 – When a unit trust is the right tool**
"A unit trust is more **flexible**. It's the right time for a unit trust when:
1. **Your goal comes before 55**: a house deposit in 5 years, children's education, starting a business.
2. **You want to invest more than RM3,000 a year** on top of PRS.
3. **You want to choose**: shares, bonds, Shariah funds, Asia or global funds.
4. **You may need the money back.** You can sell your units any time, although the value may be lower than what you put in.
In short: **unit trust = flexible, for goals before retirement.**"

**Step 4 – Many people use both**
"For many of my clients, the best answer is **both**:
- **RM3,000 a year into PRS** for the tax saving and retirement,
- **the rest into a unit trust** for other goals,
- and **EPF** as the steady base."

**Step 5 – When is the best time to start?**
"People wait for the 'perfect time' in the market. Nobody can predict it.
What works is **starting early and investing every month**. This is called **ringgit-cost averaging**: when prices are low, your money buys more units.
**Time in the market is more important than timing the market.**"

**Closing**
"So remember:
- **Safety net first**: 6 months of savings.
- **PRS** for retirement and tax saving: up to RM3,000 a year, before 31 December.
- **Unit trust** for flexible goals before retirement.
- **Start early, invest monthly.**
If you'd like, I can sit down with you and work out how much fits your income. Feel free to message me."

**Disclaimer (say or show at the end)**
"This is general information, not financial advice. Investments go up and down, and returns are not guaranteed. Please read the product highlights sheet before investing."

## Quick table for the slide / handout
| | **PRS** | **Unit trust** |
|---|---|---|
| Best for | Retirement | Goals before 55 (house, education, business) |
| Tax relief | Up to RM3,000/yr (if you pay tax) | None |
| Access | 70% locked to 55; 30% early with 8% penalty | Sell any time |
| Amount | Relief only on RM3,000 | No limit |
| When | Paying tax, before 31 Dec, long-term | Flexible goals, more than RM3,000, want choice |

## Tips when speaking
- Ask first: "Do you pay income tax? What are you saving for? When will you need it?"
- No promises of returns; use "historically / may / not guaranteed".
- For a client who pays no Malaysian tax (e.g. working in Singapore): lead with EPF / unit trust and explain PRS has no tax benefit for now.
