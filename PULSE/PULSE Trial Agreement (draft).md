The Provider lets the Advisor use the PULSE app and its tools (A-N-T fact-find, analysis slides, offer documents, social posts, reminders) for a free trial.

**2. Trial period**
From ____/____/______ to ____/____/______ (3 months). After that, use continues only if both sides agree in writing.

**3. Ownership**
PULSE – its design, code, templates, slides and logos (including "7 Financial Blind Spots") – belongs to the Provider. The Advisor gets permission to use it during the trial only. The Advisor will not copy, share, sell or change PULSE for anyone else.

**4. Client data**
Client information the Advisor enters stays in the Advisor's own Google account and belongs to the Advisor and their clients. The Advisor is responsible for handling it under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) and agency rules. If connected to a Master sheet, a copy of client data (no files) goes to the manager as agreed with the agency.

**5. End of trial**
When the trial ends (or either side stops it early with ___ days' notice):
- the Provider may switch PULSE off for the Advisor;
- the Advisor stops using PULSE and removes the PULSE code from their Google Sheet (Extensions → Apps Script) if asked;
- the Advisor keeps their own client list and folders.

**6. No guarantee**
PULSE is provided "as is". Tax/insurance figures in templates and posts are general information – the Advisor checks them before use with clients.

**7. Confidentiality**
Both sides keep each other's client information confidential.
