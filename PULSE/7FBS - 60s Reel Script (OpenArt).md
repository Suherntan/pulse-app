## 3. How to make it in OpenArt

1. **Keyframe images**: make one image per scene with your saved Daniel character and the shared look, at 9:16.
2. **Animate**: use *Image to Video* on each keyframe, 5 seconds per clip (the 6-second scenes = one 5 s clip, slowed a little or held). Put a camera move in the prompt: *slow push-in*, *orbit*, *dolly forward*.
3. **Voiceover**: use OpenArt's text-to-speech / audio tool, or ElevenLabs. Pick a warm, confident voice. Leave a 0.3 s gap before each number ("One… Two…") so the cuts land on the beat.
4. **Edit in CapCut**: line up the clips with the VO, add **big bold captions** (most people watch on mute), a "whoosh" sound on each number, and the end card with your logo.
5. **Music**: add a trending sound inside Instagram / Facebook when you post, at low volume under the VO. Trending audio from the in-app library helps reach.

## 4. Why this should do well

- **Hook in the first 2 seconds**: a question about losing money plus a cracking floor.
- **A numbered list (1–7)** keeps people watching to the end.
- **"How many did you spot?"** gets comments, which push the Reel to more people.
- **"Comment GUIDE"** turns viewers into leads. Reply with the booklet or a WhatsApp link (set up a ManyChat / Meta auto-reply on the keyword).

## 6. Before you post

- The figures (0.25% savings rate, 3.5% inflation, RM250k degree, ICU cost) come from the booklet. Check they are still current before posting.
- Keep it educational: **no insurer or product names**, and no promised returns. This matches the rule for the *7 Financial Blind Spots with Su Hern* page.
