# Investment Rules of Thumb – Summary (for choosing what goes into ANT)

- **Created:** 27 Oct 2026
- **Status:** research only – Su to pick which rules go into the A-N-T Analysis
- All are **quick estimates**, not exact maths and not advice. Returns used below are examples only.

## A. Growth & time
| Rule | How it works | Example | Fits ANT area |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rule of 72** | 72 ÷ yearly return = years to **double** | 72 ÷ 6% = 12 years | Investment, Retirement |
| **Rule of 114** | 114 ÷ return = years to **triple** | 114 ÷ 6% = 19 years | Investment |
| **Rule of 144** | 144 ÷ return = years to **x4** | 144 ÷ 6% = 24 years | Investment |
| **Reverse 72** | 72 ÷ years = return needed | Double in 10 yrs → 7.2% | Investment goal |
| **72 for inflation** | 72 ÷ inflation = years for costs to double | 72 ÷ 3% = 24 years (RM5,000 expenses → RM10,000) | Retirement, Education |
| **Compounding / start early** | Small amount for longer beats big amount later | RM300/month from 25 vs 35 | Investment |
| **Ringgit-cost averaging** | Same amount every month, buy more units when price is low | RM500/month into a fund | Investment |

## B. How to split money
| Rule | How it works | Fits ANT area |
|---|---|---|
| **100 minus age** (or **110 / 120 minus age**) | % in growth (shares / equity funds) = 100 − age; rest in safer (bonds, FD) | Investment (risk level) |
| **50 / 30 / 20** | 50% needs, 30% wants, 20% savings & investing | Cash flow (before analysis) |
| **Pay yourself first – 10–20%** | Save/invest at least 10–20% of income first | Investment |

## C. Protection (L.I.F.E)
| Rule | How it works | Fits ANT area |
|---|---|---|
| **Emergency fund 3–6 months** | Cash = 3–6 months of expenses (6+ if self-employed) | Before everything |
| **Life cover 10× income** | Death / TPD cover ≈ 10 × yearly income (MY sites; often 10–20×) | Death, Disability |
| **Critical illness 3–5× income** | CI cover = 3–5 years of income (common industry guide) | Critical Illness |
| **Insurance cost ≈ 10% of income** | Total premiums around 10% of income (guides say 6–15%) | All protection |
| **DIME** | Debt + Income (years) + Mortgage + Education = cover needed | Death |

## D. Retirement
| Rule | How it works | Fits ANT area |
|---|---|---|
| **4% rule** | Take out 4% a year from retirement savings → savings needed ≈ **25 × yearly spending** | Retirement |
| **70% rule** | Need about 70% of last income in retirement | Retirement |
