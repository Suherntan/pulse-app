PULSE – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

PULSE is a private work tool used by Tan Su Hern (insurance and financial planning advisor, Malaysia) and advisors she invites. It is not a public service.

What PULSE stores

Client details that an advisor enters (such as name, contact details and financial planning notes) are saved only in that advisor's own Google account (Google Sheets and Google Drive). PULSE does not sell or share this information.

Facebook

When an advisor connects their Facebook Page, PULSE uses Facebook's official tools only to publish posts that the advisor has written and scheduled to that Page. PULSE does not read, collect or store information about people who view or interact with the Page. The Page access key is kept in the advisor's own Google account and can be removed at any time in Facebook settings (Settings & privacy → Business integrations) or by disconnecting PULSE.

Removing your data

To remove data, contact the advisor who holds it or Tan Su Hern. Advisors can delete their data at any time by deleting the rows in their Google Sheet or the files in their Google Drive.

Contact

Tan Su Hern – please contact through the Facebook Page that published the post.
